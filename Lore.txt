The lore of Monster Hunter: World dives into a vast, evolving ecosystem filled with majestic, dangerous monsters and a world shaped by their power. Set on a large, uncharted landmass known as the New World, the story revolves around the Research Commission, an organization tasked with studying this mysterious land and its creatures. Here’s a breakdown of the lore and key story elements in Monster Hunter: World and its Iceborne expansion:

1. The New World and the Elder Crossing

In Monster Hunter: World, the New World is a continent full of ecosystems, unlike anything the Research Commission has seen. Every ten years, Elder Dragons like Zorah Magdaros make a mysterious migration from the Old World to the New World in a phenomenon called the Elder Crossing. These powerful, ancient dragons cross the ocean to the New World, and their arrival causes massive upheavals in the local ecosystems.

The Research Commission was formed to understand the purpose of the Elder Crossing and study the mysteries of this untamed land. Hunters and researchers are dispatched to the New World, forming what’s known as the Fifth Fleet, tasked with assisting the Commission’s work and uncovering the secrets of the Elder Crossing.

2. Zorah Magdaros and the Main Story

The game begins with the Fifth Fleet’s arrival and the appearance of Zorah Magdaros, a colossal, volcanic Elder Dragon. The Commission believes Zorah holds the key to understanding the Elder Crossing, as it’s the largest and most powerful Elder Dragon seen in recent history.

As players (hunters) explore the New World, they track Zorah Magdaros, aiming to contain it and prevent any ecological catastrophe from its destructive migration. This search leads the hunter through various biomes—from the Ancient Forest to the Coral Highlands and the Rotten Vale—all teeming with unique monsters adapted to their environments.

The climax of Zorah’s storyline happens when it tries to reach the Everstream, a network of energy veins under the New World. If Zorah were to die in the Everstream, its body would release massive amounts of bioenergy, potentially causing a catastrophic eruption. Hunters manage to redirect Zorah, saving the New World, but this only uncovers more questions about the Elder Dragons and the New World’s mysterious energy sources.

3. Nergigante and the Cycle of Life

As hunters delve deeper into the New World’s mysteries, they encounter Nergigante, a fearsome Elder Dragon known as the “Eater of Elders.” Unlike other monsters, Nergigante actively hunts other Elder Dragons, feeding on them and maintaining a balance in the ecosystem.

Nergigante’s existence hints at a natural cycle—an ecosystem where even the most powerful creatures are kept in check. Elder Dragons shape the world with their powers and behavior, while Nergigante keeps their population in balance, preventing any single dragon from overwhelming the ecosystem.

4. The Ancient Forest, Wildspire Waste, Coral Highlands, and Rotten Vale

Each area in the New World is its own complex ecosystem with a food chain, a climate, and specific flora and fauna. The game presents a wide array of creatures and ecosystems, each one with monsters that have evolved to survive:

Ancient Forest: A dense jungle where creatures like the Great Jagras and Rathalos reside. The forest is home to diverse life forms, making it an intricate ecosystem.

Wildspire Waste: A desert-like area with wetlands, where Barroth and Diablos roam. The harsh conditions create a landscape of predators and scavengers.

Coral Highlands: A unique, high-altitude region filled with coral-like structures and colorful creatures like the Paolumu. It’s a floating, almost surreal ecosystem.

Rotten Vale: A toxic, decomposing area where monsters feed on carcasses. The Vale is home to the Odogaron and effluvium-based life forms that thrive in its decay-filled environment.

Each of these areas illustrates the game’s theme of ecological balance, with monsters at every level of the food chain, from scavengers to apex predators.

5. Xeno’jiiva and the Energy Mystery

Towards the end of Monster Hunter: World, hunters uncover a creature called Xeno’jiiva, an infant Elder Dragon that has been absorbing bioenergy from the Everstream. Xeno’jiiva was unknowingly drawing Elder Dragons to the New World, using the energy from their death to nourish itself. The Commission realizes that the Elder Crossing was driven by Xeno’jiiva’s pull, as Elder Dragons were instinctively drawn to the New World to die, releasing energy for Xeno’jiiva’s growth.

Xeno’jiiva represents a culmination of energy that would disrupt the entire New World ecosystem if left unchecked. Hunters eventually defeat it, stopping the cycle of Elder Crossings for the time being, though the energy of the New World remains a central mystery.

6. Iceborne and the Frozen Region of Hoarfrost Reach

In Iceborne, the hunters encounter Velkhana, an ice-element Elder Dragon, and a new area called Hoarfrost Reach, an icy tundra with its own ecosystems and monsters. This marks the beginning of a new mystery around the Old Everwyrm, a creature tied to the origins of the New World’s energy flows.

The Commission realizes that Velkhana is part of a larger ecosystem in the tundra and that the creature they're actually seeking is Shara Ishvalda, a mysterious, rock-covered Elder Dragon capable of manipulating seismic energy. Shara Ishvalda is revealed to be causing disturbances across the New World by disrupting the flow of energy through the Everstream, posing a threat to the stability of the New World.

7. Themes of Coexistence and Ecological Balance

Throughout Monster Hunter: World, the game emphasizes coexistence, as the Research Commission studies the New World’s ecosystems without the intent of domination or exploitation. The hunters respect the creatures they study, understanding that every monster plays a crucial role in its environment. They aim to maintain balance, protecting the natural order from threats, even if it means facing down terrifying creatures like Nergigante or Shara Ishvalda.

The lore and story of Monster Hunter: World ultimately highlight the importance of ecosystems and how every creature, from the smallest scavenger to the mightiest Elder Dragon, is essential to the New World’s survival. The game concludes with the Research Commission and hunters respecting the New World’s mysteries, vowing to continue learning from the land and its creatures rather than dominating it.
